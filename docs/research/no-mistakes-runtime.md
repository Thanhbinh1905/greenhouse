# One-pane review: no-mistakes execution and the Posse boundary

## Answer

- **Inferred:** Reuse no-mistakes' separation of a compact display, a headless controller, and durable execution state. Do not copy its approval policy or extract its executor into Posse. Posse already owns the harder contracts: independent Review Tasks, candidate custody, exact-head evidence, bounded repairs, and audited cap Decisions. A compact client over those contracts is the smaller change. [N:internal/tui/commands.go:99-187; P:docs/spec.md:1204-1210,1239-1262]
- **Seen:** One pane does not mean one agent conversation. no-mistakes' TUI connects to a daemon; the daemon executes steps in a private worktree and invokes agent backends. A reviewer session is separate from the fixer, and post-fix review starts cold. [N:internal/cli/attach.go:44-54,155; internal/daemon/manager.go:1443-1461,1621-1696; internal/pipeline/pipeline.go:106-138]
- **Inferred:** A Posse review display should show the candidate head, stage and round budgets, findings, selected repairs, and cap choices in one terminal. Hiding execution windows must not replace the original Worker, reassign its PR, or let an author certify its own work. [P:docs/spec.md:678,774,1204-1210,1362]

## AXI, cancellation, and interrupted recovery

They stop at a gate, CI-ready point, final outcome, or the default eight-minute wait limit. Wait expiry means reattach, not cancellation; CI-ready does not mean merged. Failed/cancelled runs offer rerun; ordinary active gates do not. ## Posse guarantees a compact client must retain

- **Seen:** Posse requires a fresh Review Task/session, not a different model or Profile. Session-selector launch arguments are refused. The Reviewer never edits; the Change Worker repairs. Shared Profile/model is allowed, shared author conversation is not. [P:internal/app/review_identity.go:29-34; docs/spec.md:678,1362; internal/app/review_identity_test.go:203-207]
- **Seen:** Private candidate custody isolates validation from concurrent author edits. Promotion requires exact final head, target, config, and expected author/remote heads; author movement refuses. Managed path/ref leases exclude conflicting writers, but local execution is not an OS security sandbox. [P:docs/spec.md:1090,1204-1210,1231; internal/app/validation_candidate_promote.go:119-129,161-198]
- **Seen:** Numbered Review rounds bind scope base, head, target, baseline tree, and delta digest. Later scope removes clean target-merge changes; cumulative added-line blockers can still count. The fingerprint ledger suppresses repeats and records regressions against existing IDs. [P:internal/app/review_rounds.go:354-432; internal/store/review_findings.go:183-274; docs/spec.md:1268-1280]
- **Seen:** The cap is lifetime-bound. In admitted full-pipeline plans, Review verdict rounds and Review fix rounds share the cap; CI repairs have a separate budget. `accept-head` accepts review risk on the exact head, never supplies test/docs/lint/CI. `final-fix` permits one repair and one independent final Review; inconclusive consumes it. [P:docs/spec.md:1239-1262; internal/app/review_cap.go:17-35; internal/store/review_cap_grants.go:29-40]
- **Seen:** `rethink` starts a new Change Task through `supersedes`, retaining inherited findings and leaving the old Task unchanged. Under Project YOLO, only the Lead may record cap outcomes with `--lead-reason`; actor/reason replace no User quote. Recovery creates a new exact-head Decision without rewriting historical answers. [P:internal/app/decision_actions.go:253-279; internal/app/decisions.go:330-367; docs/proof/t1011-yolo-review-cap.md:8-33]
- **Seen:** Final-review grants and their authorizing Decisions survive snapshots independently of Notice retention. Branch authorship transfer requires a stopped previous writer, invalidates evidence, and uses generations. PR stewardship belongs to the Lead while authorship remains the Task's recorded launches. [P:docs/proof/t1011-yolo-review-cap.md:16,53; internal/store/branch_authorship.go:30-105; docs/spec.md:774]
- **Seen:** Posse merges target updates into published lineage, never rebases a pushed branch. no-mistakes' rebase and force-push custody model is therefore not a compatible transport policy. [P:docs/adr/0019-pr-merges-require-current-target.md:15,23; docs/adr/0024-built-in-validation-replaces-no-mistakes.md, “Dropped no-mistakes features”]
- **Inferred:** Moving review into the author's conversation loses fresh-session independence and separate verdict provenance. Letting both processes mutate the candidate loses stable input and writer exclusion; exact-head digests only detect that loss if independently checked at completion/promotion. Hiding panes alone loses neither guarantee. [P:internal/app/review_identity.go:29-34; docs/spec.md:1090,1204-1210,1362]

## Smallest compatible direction and open decisions

- **Inferred:** First add a compact observer/controller over existing `posse review`, `posse show`, repair selection, and Decision commands. Keep durable policy and evidence in Posse; refresh authoritative state after reconnect. Show actual budget usage and accepted risk rather than a green badge for every completed stage. No second daemon, SQLite store, or ownership system is justified by the one-pane need. [P:docs/spec.md:1161,1239-1262; N:internal/tui/commands.go:240-273]
- **Inferred:** If pane-free execution is required, introduce a headless execution option behind the existing Review Task contract, not an inline author tool call. Preserve fresh session, read-only candidate access, exact-head completion checks, author-owned repairs, cancellation, and durable verdicts. The inspected Posse contracts do not by themselves prove this new launch path exists. [P:docs/spec.md:678,1204-1210,1362]
- **Seen:** Neither inspected executor is a standalone stateless review library. no-mistakes `StepContext` directly contains `db.Run`, `db.Repo`, config, DB, worktree, and session resources. Posse round admission/rendering requires a Change Task, Project, frozen plan, Task directory, and store. [N:internal/pipeline/pipeline.go:16-138; P:internal/app/review_rounds.go:99-167,250-292,536-574]
- **Inferred:** A standalone tool could accept repository/base/head, intent, budget, and findings records without Posse Task IDs, but it would need its own run identity, custody, persistence, and audit adapter. Reusing schemas and review semantics is plausible; claiming existing Posse guarantees without that adapter is not. Extraction is unwarranted until independent standalone use is a firm requirement. [P:internal/app/review_rounds.go:250-292,536-574; N:internal/pipeline/pipeline.go:16-138]
- **Inferred:** The remaining product choices are whether one-pane means hidden execution or no Worker/Review panes at all; whether standalone use is required; and which repair/cap actions the UI exposes under existing authority. Technical risks are stale actions after reconnect, duplicate dispatch, candidate writes during review, crash-after-repair evidence, and headless backend session leakage. Existing exact-head checks, grant idempotency, and fresh-session rules must remain authoritative. [P:docs/spec.md:678,1204-1210,1239-1262; N:internal/tui/commands.go:240-347]
